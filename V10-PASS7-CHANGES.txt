Ancient Innovations Control App v10 Pass 7 — Simplified Fulfillment

- Fulfillment is now a working to-do list only.
- Orders needing production do not appear in Fulfillment.
- A finished-goods barcode scan is the handoff from Production to Fulfillment.
- A uniquely matching pending Show Order is marked production-complete and Ready to Fulfill when its finished item is scanned.
- Removed manual Pick / Picked / Pack / Ready to Ship / Complete buttons from Fulfillment.
- Fulfillment shows only physically-ready orders without tracking.
- TrackingNumber is the automatic exit gate: once future Pirate Ship sync writes tracking, the order no longer appears in the active queue.
- Existing order/history records are retained; nothing is deleted.
