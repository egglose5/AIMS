Ancient Innovations Control App v10 Pass 8 — Automatic Order Routing & Reservation

- Existing finished stock can satisfy a Show Order automatically.
- Exact, unambiguous product matches are reserved from shelf inventory and released directly to Fulfillment.
- If no matching stock exists, the order remains a Customer Order in Production.
- Production now separates Customer Orders from Inventory Replacement work.
- A finished barcode scan is still the only production completion action.
- When a completion scan satisfies a customer order, the scanned unit is immediately reserved to that order so it is not counted as free shelf stock.
- Low-stock replacement now triggers below 5 available units (4 or fewer) and remains latched until stock is restored to 10.
- No manual routing/status step was added.
- Conservative safety rule: automatic stock routing only occurs when exactly one initialized physical SKU matches the order. Ambiguous matches stay in Production rather than guessing.
