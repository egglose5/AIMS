Mustaine-AI v9 — KISS Production

Production now answers two questions only:
1) What sold and still needs to be remade?
2) What initialized notebook artwork is low on the shelf?

Rules locked from shop workflow:
- All Square sales feed Production automatically.
- Seller comes from Square; unassigned sales display as Online.
- No Start Job, In Progress, Ready, machine routing, stitcher tracking, or manual production queue.
- Finished product is scanned once; that scan adds one unit to FINISHED_SHELF inventory.
- A finished scan pays down the oldest outstanding sale for that initialized variation.
- Low stock is based on combined Black + Brown inventory for an artwork.
- Low-stock latch starts when combined inventory reaches 5 or fewer and stays until it is back to 10.
- Black/Brown counts are displayed; the software does not decide what leather color to make.
- Products without artwork display the product name and quantity only.
- No manual Add Production action.
- Machine Control removed from Production.
- Stitch Orders and Square Control removed from normal navigation; underlying pages/data remain intact.
- Initial Inventory navigation label shortened to Inventory.
- COGS plumbing is left intact and outside the production workflow.

Production automatically refreshes the last 30 days of Square sales when opened.
