Ancient Innovations Control App v10 Pass 8.2
Customer-order routing repair + production cleanup

- Production now self-hydrates pending Square Show Orders into the shared fulfillment backbone.
  The user no longer has to open Show Orders first for customer production work to appear.
- Repairs unshipped Show Orders left in prototype/intermediate statuses when no real inventory
  reservation exists, returning them to Customer Order Production safely.
- Existing orders already handed off by a real ORDER_RESERVATION are preserved and are not
  pushed backward.
- Square shipping/service lines (including USPS Shipping) are excluded from production needs.
- The Needs Made metric now includes physical pieces needed for customer-order production as
  well as inventory-replacement pieces.
- No database wipe. No schema changes. Existing fulfillment and inventory records are preserved.
