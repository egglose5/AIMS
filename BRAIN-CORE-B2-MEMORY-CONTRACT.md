# Ancient Innovations Brain Core B2 — Memory & Learning Contract

B2 adds Ancient Innovations-owned institutional memory. It deliberately separates:

- FACT — a sourced current business fact.
- EPISODE — something that happened.
- PROCEDURE — how Ancient Innovations intentionally works.
- LESSON — a reviewed durable inference.
- ASSUMPTION — an uncertain belief that remains visibly uncertain.
- DECISION — recommendation, human choice/reasoning, and later outcome.
- LEARNING CANDIDATE — proposed lesson that is not durable until reviewed.
- CONTRADICTION — conflicting memories that remain open until explicitly resolved.

Rules:
1. Agent observations do not automatically become durable lessons.
2. Human overrides store the reasoning, not merely the final yes/no.
3. Outcomes remain linked to prior recommendations and decisions.
4. Conflicting knowledge is surfaced, not silently overwritten.
5. Every durable memory keeps provenance, scope, confidence, and status.
6. Control App/Arm-owned operational records remain the source of truth.
7. Hermes/model providers do not own or directly mutate Brain memory storage.
